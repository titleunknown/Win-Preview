Win-Preview v0.3 adds a much better crop tool, optional tabs, and a Zoom tool with drag-to-pan.

## Download

**Win-Preview-v0.3-win-x64.zip** is for Windows 10 (1809+) and Windows 11, x64. Unzip it anywhere and run `WinPreview.exe`. Nothing needs to be installed; .NET and the Windows App SDK are included. To update from v0.2, unzip over the old folder or into a new one.

> The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first launch. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. The SHA-256 checksum is in `Win-Preview-v0.3-win-x64.zip.sha256`.

## New in v0.3

**Crop**
- Press **C** (or the Crop button), drag over the area to keep, then adjust it with the handles or move it. The rest of the picture is dimmed, with a thirds grid and a live size readout. Enter or the floating **Crop** button applies it.
- An **Aspect ratio** bar appears while you crop: Free, Same as page, 1:1, 4:5, 5:4, 2:3, 3:2, 3:4, 4:3, 9:16, 16:9, plus a button to swap portrait and landscape.
- On PDFs, **All pages** crops every page to the same area, and **Reset Crop** (More menu) restores the full page. Works on rotated pages.

**Viewing**
- **Zoom tool:** press **Z** (or the magnifier button). Double-click to jump to 100% around the point you clicked; double-click again to return to fit. When the picture is larger than the window the cursor becomes a hand and dragging pans.
- The normal Select tool keeps the standard arrow cursor.

**Windows and tabs**
- Optional **tabs**: turn on **Open Files in Tabs** in the More menu to open files as tabs in one window. Each tab keeps its own zoom, position, sidebar and unsaved changes. Ctrl+Tab switches, Ctrl+W closes, and right-click a tab to move it to its own window.
- The whole window now accepts dropped files (not just the middle), with a highlight while dragging.
- The window can't be made smaller than 780 × 480.

**Fixes**
- A leftover empty message bar could stay on screen after a notice closed.
